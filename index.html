Dishu, kabhi-kabhi main sochta hoon ki tumhe exactly kaise bataun ki tum mere liye kitni special ho… kyunki jitna feel karta hoon, utna shayad words mein kabhi explain hi nahi kar paunga. ❤️

Tum meri life mein bas ek person banke nahi aayi… tum dheere-dheere meri life ka woh beautiful sa part ban gayi ho jiske bina ab bahut si cheezein incomplete si lagti hain. Tumse baat karna, tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein sunna, tumhara care karna, mera mood samajh lena… ye sab mere liye ordinary nahi hai, bohot precious hai. 🥹

"Mujhe tumhari woh smile pasand hai… kyunki jab tum genuinely smile karti ho, toh mujhe andar se ek ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai."

Kabhi main apni feelings properly express nahi kar pata, kabhi words kam pad jaate hain… but please, kabhi meri silence ko meri feelings mat samajhna. Mere dil mein tumhare liye jo jagah hai na, woh kisi ek reaction, conversation ya bad day se kabhi change nahi hoti.

Aur ek cheez… tumhe kabhi perfect banne ki zarurat nahi hai. Tum jaisi ho, jis tarah feel karti ho, jis tarah care karti ho… mujhe bas wahi Dishu pasand hai. ❤️

"Kuch connections ko naam dena zaroori nahi hota, bas unhe dil se feel karna enough hota hai."

Tum important ho. Tum special ho. Tumhari smile important hai. Aur sabse zyada… tum mere liye important ho. 🫶🏻
