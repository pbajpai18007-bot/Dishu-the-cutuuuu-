Hey Dishu

Kuch cheezein words se zyada dil se feel hoti hain… ek chhota sa corner sirf tumhare liye.

Created with pure heart

For Dishu ❤️

Jiske smile mein mujhe sukoon milta hai.

Dishu, kabhi-kabhi main sochta hoon ki tumhe exactly kaise bataun ki tum mere liye kitni special ho… kyunki jitna feel karta hoon, utna shayad words mein kabhi explain hi nahi kar paunga. ❤️

Tum meri life mein bas ek person banke nahi aayi… tum dheere-dheere meri life ka woh beautiful sa part ban gayi ho jiske bina ab bahut si cheezein incomplete si lagti hain. Tumse baat karna, tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein sunna, tumhara care karna, mera mood samajh lena… ye sab mere liye ordinary nahi hai. Mere liye ye sab bahut precious hai. 🥹

"Mujhe tumhari woh smile pasand hai… kyunki jab tum genuinely smile karti ho, toh mujhe andar se ek ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai."

Kabhi main apni feelings properly express nahi kar pata, kabhi words kam pad jaate hain… but please, kabhi meri silence ko meri feelings mat samajhna. Mere dil mein tumhare liye jo jagah hai na, woh kisi ek conversation ya bad day se kabhi change nahi hoti.

Tumne jis tarah mujhe samjha hai, support kiya hai aur hamesha stand liya hai, uske liye thank you bohot chhota word hai. Aur haan… tumhe kabhi perfect banne ki zarurat nahi hai. Tum jaisi ho, mujhe bas wahi Dishu pasand hai. ❤️

"Agar kabhi lage ki tum akeli ho, toh bas ek baar mujhe yaad kar lena… tumhari baat sunne ke liye main hamesha rahunga."

Kuch connections ko naam dena zaroori nahi hota, bas unhe dil se feel karna enough hota hai. Thank you for being you, Dishu. 🫶🏻
